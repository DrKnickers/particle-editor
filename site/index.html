Particle effects editor · Empire at War

Effects, previewed in game context.

An updated .alo particle editor that previews effects with game context: a game unit for scale, a skydome and ground for backdrop, your mod stack for assets, and more.

↓ Download for Windows View the source

Windows x64 · uses your game install for full context

1 Scene context

Reference the scene.

Drop in a game unit for scale, load a skydome and ground, and tune the particle against that scene.

2 Mod stack

Edit against your mod and submods.

Set the active mod stack and its order. The editor resolves the same asset overrides as your Empire at War install — models, textures, and shaders.

3 Spawner

Preview effects in flight.

Fire the effect as a repeating burst, or launch it along an arc to see how your effect reads in motion.

4 Emitter order

Reorder emitters with ease.

Drag and drop emitters and preview the new draw order in the live viewport.

5 Atlas picker

Pick atlas frames as images.

Browse an atlas as a grid of frames and click the one you want.

6 Toolset

The rest of the toolset.

Smaller tools and safeguards, grouped by what they're for.

Authoring

Link groups

Edit one emitter; linked members get the same change.

Stacked curves

See every channel at once — read green while editing red.

Import emitters

Bring in some or all of the emitters in from another .alo.

Texture picker

Browse colour and bump-map textures from a recent and pinned palette.

Preview & viewport

Lighting

Adjust sun, fill, and ambient; preview shaders that respond to light.

Pause + frame-step

Freeze and step the simulation to inspect fine movement.

Reload live

Reload textures and shaders without restarting — iterate on custom shaders and textures live.

Files & reliability

Atomic saves

A failed save leaves your existing .alo intact.

Crash recovery

Autosave restores your session after a crash.

Undo everything

Edits, gizmo drags, and reorders share one undo history.

Also included

  • Light and dark themes
  • Full keyboard navigation
