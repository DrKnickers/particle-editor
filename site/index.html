Particle effects editor — Empire at War

Effects, previewed in game context.

Author .alo particle systems with the references that make tuning practical: game units for scale, skydomes for backdrop, mod stacks for assets, and a live viewport beside the controls.

↓ Download for Windows View the source

Windows x64 · uses your game install for full context

1 Scene Context

Judge scale before you launch the game.

Bring in an in-game unit as a reference, load a skydome, and tune the particle against the scene it belongs in.

2 Authoring Workflow

Reorder emitters by feel.

Drag emitters into the order you need, inspect the properties that matter, and keep timing, color, and alpha curves close to the live viewport.

3 Atlas Picker

Pick frames visually.

Browse atlas frames as images instead of filenames — each frame previews the way your emitter's blend mode actually renders it, with empty frames dimmed out — so you pick the texture slice that fits the effect you are building.

4 Mod Stack Awareness

Work in the stack you actually ship.

Pick the active mod, keep layered assets visible, and edit against the same content assumptions your Empire at War setup will load.
