Particle effects editor · Empire at War

Edit and preview Empire at War particle effects.

Open .alo files, load models, textures, and shaders through an ordered mod stack, and preview the result with a skydome, ground, lighting, and a movable reference object.

Windows x64 · uses data from your Empire at War installation

1 Interface

A rebuilt interface

The editor now uses resizable panels, docked tools, and light and dark themes. The emitter tree, properties, curves, and live preview remain visible as the workspace changes around the task.

2 Viewport

Scene references

Load a game unit or structure for scale, then choose a skydome, ground, and lighting for the preview.

3 Assets

Ordered mod loading

Add mod and submod folders to the active stack and drag them into order. The editor checks the top layer first when loading models, textures, and shaders.

4 Preview

Spawner controls

Launch the effect once or at a fixed interval. Set its direction and speed to inspect it in motion.

5 Emitter tree

Emitter reordering

Drag root emitters to a new position. The viewport updates immediately so you can check the resulting draw order.

6 Textures

Atlas frame picker

Open the texture as a frame grid and click a cell to assign that frame to the selected Index keys.

7 Other tools

Other editing and recovery tools

Controls that do not need a full walkthrough.

Authoring

Link groups

Share selected parameters across a group of emitters; changing one updates the others.

Curve visibility

Compare multiple channels on one canvas; checkboxes control which curves are visible.

Import emitters

Select and import individual emitters or whole branches from another .alo.

Frequently used textures

Choose colour and bump maps from per-mod Pinned and Recent thumbnail lists.

Preview & viewport

Lighting

Adjust the sun, fill lights, ambient light, shadows, and bloom in the preview.

Pause and frame-step

Pause the simulation, then advance one or ten frames at a time.

Reload assets

Reload textures and shaders without restarting the editor.

Files & reliability

Atomic saves

A failed save leaves your existing .alo intact.

Crash recovery

After an interrupted session, choose a recent or stable autosave when the editor opens.

Undo and redo

Editor changes, gizmo moves, and emitter reorders share one undo history.

Also included

  • Light and dark themes
  • Full keyboard navigation
